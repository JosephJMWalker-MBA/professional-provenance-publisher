Scientific AI Governance Thesis

In R&D, preserving raw files is necessary but not sufficient. AI-assisted cleaning, labeling, classification, schema mapping, summarization, and hypothesis generation can change the usable scientific dataset or cause interpretation to acquire the apparent status of evidence. The design goal is to keep observed evidence, deterministic transformation, human interpretation, AI inference, hypothesis, and validation state visibly distinct and auditable throughout the research lifecycle.
